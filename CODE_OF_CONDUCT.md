# Code of Conduct

Emdeck welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. Keep
issues, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces respectful and useful.

- Discuss the work and explain disagreements with evidence.
- Be patient with newcomers and considerate of other people's time.
- Respect privacy; share only public or synthetic examples and sanitized logs.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, insult, spam, or publish private
  information.

The [maintainers](GOVERNANCE.md#maintainers) may edit or remove harmful content,
close disruptive threads, warn participants, or restrict participation. They
consider severity and repeated behavior and explain moderation decisions where
doing so does not expose private information.

Use GitHub's **Report content** action for abusive posts. For private concerns,
use a contact method a maintainer makes available on their GitHub profile. Do
not publish personal details in an issue to report misconduct. Security
vulnerabilities use the separate [private reporting process](SECURITY.md).
